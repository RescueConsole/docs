# Microchip lookup

Enter a microchip number to see whether any rescue on the RescueConsole network has registered that chip, and whether there is an active safety flag shared with the network. Anyone can use this. No account needed.

Microchip number

Look it up

Digits, with or without spaces or dashes.

## What this page will not do

It looks up microchips only. Searching the registry by a person's name, email address or phone number stays inside a rescue's own account, because a public page that answered those would be a way to read the list one address at a time, and the people named on it never agreed to that.

What you see here is a summary: that a flag exists, its kind and how serious it is. The account of what happened, and the rescue that filed it, are for the rescues who share the registry with one another.
