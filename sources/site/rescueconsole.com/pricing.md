### However you take the money

A dollar an adoption, whether it arrives through us, through a card reader, in cash at an adoption day, or through an app we never see. We do not charge more for payments we do not handle.

### And whoever pays it

Carry the dollar yourself, or add it to the adoption fee so the adopter covers it, shown as its own line at checkout, never as a surprise. One setting, either way.

## What that comes to

- **Twelve adoptions in a year:** $12, and nothing at all in the first year.
- **Forty a month:** $40 that month. A quieter month costs less, not the same.
- **A month with none:** nothing.

We record what is owed each month and close the month when it ends. Nothing is taken from a card automatically.

## Questions people ask

What counts as an adoption?

An adoption you complete and do not reverse. If a placement comes back to you and you record the reversal, it is not billed, and if it was already on an invoice the next invoice credits it back. A month is invoiced on the 5th of the month after it, so an adoption you enter a few days late still lands in the month it happened. Fosters, transfers and returns are not adoptions and are never counted.

Can the adopter pay the dollar instead of us?

Yes, and it is a setting rather than a conversation. Turn it on and the dollar is added to the adoption fee at checkout as its own line, so the adopter sees exactly what it is, and that adoption comes off your invoice. Turn it off and it stays with you. It is the same dollar either way.

Does it cost more if we take payments somewhere else?

No. A dollar an adoption is a dollar an adoption whether the money came through us, through a card reader, in cash at an adoption day, by cheque, or through an app we never see. We do not charge more for payments we do not process, and we never will. You should be free to take money the way that suits you and your adopters.

What if we place no animals in a month?

You owe nothing that month. There is no minimum, no monthly fee and nothing to cancel. A quiet month costs what it should.

Is the free year really the whole product?

Yes, and that is why we do not call it a trial. Every workspace, your own domain, your public site, unlimited staff and volunteer accounts, for the rest of the month you sign up in and the twelve full months after it, so joining on the 30th costs you nothing. Nothing switches off at the end of it. The first bill simply arrives.

Is every workspace really included?

Yes. People, Organizations, Animals, Fundraising, Shifts, Events, Communications, Public Registry, plus forms, workflows, documents, the calendar, your public website and the settings behind them. There is one plan, so there is no feature somebody else has paid more for.

Do volunteers count as accounts?

No. A volunteer who signs in to take shifts is not a staff account, and there is no limit on how many you have.

Can I bring my existing data?

Animals, people, donations and events import from a spreadsheet with column mapping, in your free year and after it. Every list exports to CSV whenever you want it back.

What happens when the free year ends?

Nothing changes and nothing is deleted. You carry on, and the adoptions you complete from then on are billed at a dollar each.
