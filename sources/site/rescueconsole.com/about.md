About

# Built for the people running rescues, not just the software

Most animal rescues run on a patchwork: a spreadsheet for intake, a separate donation platform, a sign-up sheet for volunteers, and an inbox for everything else. Every seam between those tools is a place where an adopter’s history gets lost, a donor gets thanked twice, or a volunteer’s hours never get counted.

RescueConsole exists to close those seams. It is one operational record, for every animal, every adopter, every donor and every volunteer, shared across the workspaces a rescue actually runs day to day: people, organizations, animals, fundraising, shifts, events, communications and the public registry. Around them sit the forms people fill in, the workflows a team follows, and the public website that is filled in from the same data.

We built it for staff who would rather spend their time with animals and adopters than reconciling spreadsheets, and for the organizations that need every donor dollar and every volunteer hour accounted for.

## How we work

We run on Cloudflare’s network, so there is no server for a rescue to keep up. Every list exports to CSV, because your data is yours. And we publish what has shipped and what has not: the [changelog](https://rescueconsole.com/changelog) is written for the people who run rescues, and the[roadmap](https://rescueconsole.com/roadmap) carries no dates, on purpose.

If you run a rescue and something here does not fit the way you work, [tell us](https://rescueconsole.com/contact). What rescues ask for is most of how the roadmap gets written.
